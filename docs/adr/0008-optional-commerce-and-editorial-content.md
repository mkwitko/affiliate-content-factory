# ADR 0008: Optional commerce binding and editorial content

**Status:** Proposed (owner approval pending). **Date:** 2026-09-25 (revised after the
spec-driven documentation pass, same day).
**Inputs:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-money-maker-os-audit-and-backlog.md`
(risk 1.6.1), the Content Revenue OS handoff, the ChatGPT second-pass and
spec-driven reviews (2026-09-25), the umbrella design
Content Revenue OS umbrella design  (design note removed)
and its slice specs.

## Context

`ContentItem` was modelled as "a post about one Mercado Livre offer". The
offer and its `CommercialSnapshot` are the item's identity: `offerId`,
`offerSnapshotId` and `sourceFactKeys` are required
(`packages/core/src/content/content.ts`, `schema.prisma` `content_items`),
`createVariant` and `validateVariantForReview` require an active offer, and
`ContentApproval` binds to `offerSnapshotId`. The owner requires editorial
content (YouTube long-form and derivatives) with no offer, and later
editorial content that carries one offer as an additional layer. The
creative pipeline merged on 2026-09-25 added `sourcePictureAssetIds` and
`creativeSlides` to the item; both depend on a product and therefore on an
offer.

## Decision

1. The commerce binding becomes optional (0..1). `ContentItem.offerId`,
   `ContentItem.offerSnapshotId`, `ContentVariant.offerSnapshotId` and
   `ContentApproval.offerSnapshotId` become nullable.
2. Presence of an offer is the only mode switch. `offerId != null` keeps
   every current guarantee byte for byte (offer in workspace and active,
   snapshot valid, `sourceFactKeys ⊆ snapshot.facts`,
   `snapshotStillBacksContent` at creation, review and publish, affiliate
   URL rules, commercial preview warnings, publish-time freshness gate,
   creative render).
3. `offerId == null` implies `offerSnapshotId == null`, `sourceFactKeys ==
   []` and no `sourcePictureAssetIds` (database CHECK constraints and domain
   invariants). No commercial validator, disclosure, CTA, evidence age,
   destination fingerprint or affiliate link rule runs for such content.
4. No new content tree (no `ContentProject`); no mode column (`editorial |
   commerce | hybrid` is derived from offer presence, later from binding
   count).
5. Editorial content cannot be published externally until the final
   editorial review gate exists: `publish_content` and
   `record_manual_publication` return `EDITORIAL_PUBLICATION_NOT_ENABLED`
   and the worker rejects any such attempt. The gate is lifted per channel
   kind by the slice that defines that kind's review (YouTube
   `private`/`unlisted` in S4; `public` only after an owner-approved smoke
   test).
6. Approval will bind the whole publication payload through a versioned,
   canonical `publicationFingerprint` (S2) before any YouTube provider is
   enabled; approvals created before that migration keep the old
   semantics until the variant re-enters review (no `legacy` sentinel).
7. Editorial grounding lives in versioned, immutable scripts whose claims
   reference persisted evidence only: `{ kind: "commercial_fact",
   snapshotId, key }` (unique by schema) or `{ kind: "research_observation",
   observationId }` (the `ResearchObservation` uuid, which the domain and
   repository will expose; `snapshotId + key` is not unique because the
   schema's unique key includes `sourceUrl`). Arbitrary URLs are not
   representable. A draft item may exist before any research; the barriers
   are script approval and the final editorial review, never item creation.
8. `ContentScript` rows are immutable after insert except one transition
   `draft → approved` that writes `approvedBy`, `approvedAt`,
   `approvedChecksum` once; revisions are new versions.
9. Asset commercial rights are tri-state (`commercialUse: true | false |
   null`, default `null` = unknown) with rules per provenance; publication
   to YouTube requires `true` for every required asset. "Generated by AI"
   never implies rights.
10. Multi-offer content (0..n) is a later slice (`content_commerce_bindings`),
    added only when a multi-product piece is produced; the migration path
    from 0..1 is documented in the
    S9 evolution note  (design note removed).
11. The following stay out of the schema until a server-side consumer
    exists: `monetization[]`, `productionStrategy`, `policyProfile`,
    `hypothesis`, `researchSnapshotIds[]`, a values charter, a `Brand`
    entity, a `youtube` research provider. `Niche` remains the editorial
    and brand-context container for the MVP; this is a current choice, not
    a domain invariant.

## Consequences

- S1 is one additive migration (four nullable columns, two CHECK
  constraints), no backfill, rollback possible while no editorial row
  exists; every reader of the three columns handles `null` (list in the
  S1 spec §4.5).
- Dashboard review queue and pending list show editorial variants without
  offer fields instead of hiding them.
- The affiliate flow, its skill and its tests are unchanged.
- The YouTube provider (Post For Me) is never a bypass of the editorial
  review: S3 defines and contract-tests the adapter but makes no external
  YouTube publication call; S4 enables `private`/`unlisted` only after its script,
  rights and fingerprint-bound editorial review gates pass.
